   <section class="bench">
   <p>Accelerated molecular dynamics covers Al adatom on Al(100) at 150 K and Al adatom on Al(100) at 250 K. The recorded metrics include Percent Uncertainty, Thermostat, True rate, and Uncertainty. Al adatom on Al(100) at 150 K has 1 runs and Al adatom on Al(100) at 250 K has 1 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>

   <p>Best is the lowest cost for that code on that problem. Median is the median of that code's costs. PAR-2 is the mean cost of the runs. A run that finishes counts as the calls it used.
