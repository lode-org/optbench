Lennard-Jones 38 transition-state separation has 51 runs, LJ38 double-ended saddle has 50 runs, and Pt heptamer island has 59 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>
