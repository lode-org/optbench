   <dt>Potential</dt><dd>eam (eam_al)</dd>
   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>Note</dt><dd>A start is one barrier window. The window closes when each reference barrier below 0.4 eV has been matched once from the processtable. The ranked cost counts every force evaluation: dimer images, minimizations, and prefactor calls. The stop is the maximum force on any atom. The potential is eam_al.</dd>
   <dt>Contract</dt><dd>The columns are the keys shared by every archived file.</dd>
   </dl>
   <h3>Metrics</h3>
   <p>The main metric is barrier force calls.</p>
   <p>2014 half-even mean of the starts under the cap. Failures are left out of that mean. The row keeps that mean as its published number.</p>
   <ul>
   <li>barrier force calls</li>
   <li>barrier jobs</li>
   <li>rate force calls</li>
   <li>rate jobs</li>
   </ul>
   <p>Select a column heading to sort the table.</p>
   <p>Rows are ranked by cost inside each method family and on one overall table. A run short of the full set, or a run that is not the stock method, stays off the table.</p>
   <p class="bench-gaps">A run short of the full set, or a run that is not the stock method, stays off the ranked table.</p>
