   <section class="bench">
   <p>Transition state optimization covers H2 dissociation on a fixed Cu substrate, Lennard-Jones 38, and Pt heptamer island. The recorded metrics include Force calls, Fewest force calls, Most force calls, and Median force calls. H2 dissociation on a fixed Cu substrate has 4 runs, Lennard-Jones 38 has 200 runs, and Pt heptamer island has 49 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>
