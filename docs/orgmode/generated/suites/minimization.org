   <section class="bench">
   <p>Minimization covers Lennard-Jones 38 clusters, Lennard-Jones 38 clusters with Gaussian force noise, and Morse bulk. The recorded metrics include Force calls, Most force calls, and Fewest force calls. Lennard-Jones 38 clusters has 1000 runs, Lennard-Jones 38 clusters with Gaussian force noise has 1000 runs, and Morse bulk has 100 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>
