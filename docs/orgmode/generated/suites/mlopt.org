   <section class="bench">
   <p>Machine learning optimization covers EMT Pt 38 nanoparticle and Ethene. The recorded metrics include Mean epochs, Median epochs, Mean energy RMSE, Median energy RMSE, Mean force RMSE, and Median force RMSE. EMT Pt 38 nanoparticle has 50 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>

   <p>Best is the lowest cost for that code on that problem. Median is the median of that code's costs. PAR-2 is the mean cost of the runs. A run that finishes counts as the calls it used.
