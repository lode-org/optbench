   <section class="bench">
   <p>Global optimization covers Binary Lennard-Jones 100, Lennard-Jones 150, Lennard-Jones 38 global minimum, Lennard-Jones 75, Lennard-Jones 98, TIP4P 10, TIP4P 15, and TIP4P 20. The recorded metrics include Average energy, Lowest energy, Highest energy, Mean basin-hopping steps, Basin-hopping step spread, and Mean CPU time. Binary Lennard-Jones 100 has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 150 has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 38 global minimum has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 75 has 200 runs, Lennard-Jones 98 has 100 runs, TIP4P 10 has 10000 runs, TIP4P 15 has 500 runs, and TIP4P 20 has 100 runs. An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap. A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.</p>
