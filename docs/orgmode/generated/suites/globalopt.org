   <section class="bench">
   <p>Global optimization covers Binary Lennard-Jones 100, Lennard-Jones 150, Lennard-Jones 38 global minimum, Lennard-Jones 75, Lennard-Jones 98, TIP4P 10, TIP4P 15, and TIP4P 20. The recorded metrics include avg energy, min energy, max energy, basin hopping steps mean, basin hopping steps stddev, and cpu time mean. Binary Lennard-Jones 100 has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 150 has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 38 global minimum has 100 runs, Lennard-Jones 75 has 200 runs, Lennard-Jones 98 has 100 runs, TIP4P 10 has 10000 runs, TIP4P 15 has 500 runs, and TIP4P 20 has 100 runs. Archived means follow this rule: 2014 half-even mean of the starts under the cap. Failures are left out of that mean. The row keeps that mean as its published number.</p>
